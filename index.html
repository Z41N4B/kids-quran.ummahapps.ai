About Kids Qur’an (for grown-ups)

Kids Qur’an is a free app that helps children learn and memorise parts of the Qur’an by listening and repeating, with the recitation of Sheikh Saad al-Ghamdi.

Questions

Is Kids Qur’an free?

Yes. It is free and needs no sign-up. It runs in your web browser.

Which surahs can my child learn?

Al-Fatiha, Ayatul Kursi, Al-Asr, Al-Kafirun, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas, Al-Kawthar and Al-Insan. Al-Insan is learned in three parts that unlock in turn.

How does it help children memorise?

Each part plays the recitation of Sheikh Saad al-Ghamdi. Children can repeat a part 1, 3, 5 or 10 times, play it slowly, hide the words to test themselves and turn on the English translation.
